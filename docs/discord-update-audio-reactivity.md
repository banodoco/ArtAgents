# Discord Update - Audio Reactivity Highlights

Hey everyone, in keeping with my promise to spam you, I'm sharing some more competition entries. I was thinking that a bunch of them did really interesting things with audio reactivity - both subtle and explicit - so I figured I'd share a few different examples from this angle.

I'd hugely appreciate if you spend some time going through [the entries](https://arcagidan.com?referrer=POM) and leaving votes and comments for the work that you like. Please follow the links I share - if you go after watching one of the videos I share here, please follow the link so I can account for that in the voting tallying.

---

**1. Temporal Drift - emmacatnip**

This piece by emmacatnip has so many different elements interacting, including the audio, but somehow manages to feel coherent despite this chaos - which I think is an extremely hard thing to do.

https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1316024582041243668/1490071972430745820/emmacatnip.mp4?ex=69d2b92a&is=69d167aa&hm=2609b74b06a461e41a8730bc21933abd5a6653db2edc247d693d6503e5cd58ed&

**2. Archive 113391 - yuvraj108c**

Here, Yuvraj used the audio reactivity to tell a second story layered on top of the main one. The video kind of dances between them in time with the music and it's excellently done.

https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1316024582041243668/1490075690157211758/yuvraj108c.mp4?ex=69d2bca0&is=69d16b20&hm=7b8fb568473ef808a23b642db5fc89ccbf8314527746aa445e4e2db570836a27&

**3. Cherrybomb (Original Music) - sagansagansagans**

This piece by sagans combines a traditional narrative structure with an abstract, audio-driven transformation stage - which kind of extracts you out of the main story into the emotional depth it's trying to communicate. This combination works really nicely.

https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1316024582041243668/1490075735669477406/sagansagansagans.mp4?ex=69d2bcab&is=69d16b2b&hm=bba2dcddfc32d83e97a1d15e6d69d08df755af73f552b4aab27b2820c2fd7bd2&

**4. Synth Study #1 - gorkulus**

These Windows Media Player 2030-esque visuals, in this example by gorkulus, soothe my ADD brain.

https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1316024582041243668/1490076584764506413/gorkulus.mp4?ex=69d2bd76&is=69d16bf6&hm=d948a9ccf099cfe4be6d77fe5670393811efafc3c19e524e135b2622b50b214d&

**5. Everyone All at Once - visualfrisson**

This by visualfrisson is typically incredibly impressive - not just technically, but also how it uses the technical trick to address the theme really powerfully. I think it shows off the endless, timeless quality to hip-hop lyrics that we might not always appreciate.
