## Report

**Filtergraph assembly.** `build_composite_command` emits one ffmpeg invocation: input `[0]` is a full-length `color=c=black:s=WxH:r=fps:d=<total_seconds>` base; each layer is `-i`'d next, with `-c:v libvpx-vp9` forced **before** alpha inputs (verified: ffmpeg 7.1's native VP9 decoder drops the alpha plane — `top.webm` probes as `yuv420p` yet decodes RGBA only under the libvpx decoder). Per layer (z ascending): `[N:v]format=yuva420p,` (alpha only) `scale=W:H:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=W:H:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2,setsar=1,fps=<num>/<den>,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[,format=rgba,colorchannelmixer=aa=<op>]` then chained `[prev][tN]overlay=0:0:format=auto:eof_action=pass`. Output: `format=yuv420p`, libx264, AAC, `-video_track_timescale`, `-movflags +faststart`.

**Short-layer padding.** The brief's default-`repeat` assumption was false: with `eof_action=repeat` a short top layer freezes its last frame for the rest of the plan. I verified empirically that `eof_action=pass` is the correct mechanism — when a layer's frames end, the accumulated result below passes through, so a 5-frame top over a 10-frame plan paints green at frames 0–4 and red at 5–9. This pads z=0 too (base color shows after a short bottom ends), satisfying the oracle note. Audio: lowest-z layer with an audio stream is mapped through `aformat=<plan sr/layout>,apad`; none found → `anullsrc` + apad; both pinned by `-t <total_seconds>` (validated at 30000/1001: exactly 30 frames).
