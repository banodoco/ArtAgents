## Verified facts
- `cut/run.py:368` & `resume.py:165` are **real bugs**: `astrid/packs/video_editing/executors/` contains only `cut/`; `from ..render.run import render` raises `ModuleNotFoundError` whenever `--render` is passed.
- `--renderer` accepts only `remotion`; declared in argparse, **not** in `executor.yaml`, and never reaches `render()` (which has `engine=`, not `renderer=`). Metadata-only.
- Provenance sidecar (`{out}.provenance.json`, payload `"output": out_path`) is written by render; `iteration_video` moves `hype.mp4` but leaves `hype.mp4.provenance.json` — **stale `output` field, missing `iteration.mp4.provenance.json`**; not in `OUTPUT_FILES`.
- `executor_argv` resolves `.py` stems by `metadata.pipeline_step`, qualified ids by registry; result cached process-wide, keyed only by name — `load_default_registry()` is *not* cached, so a mid-process fork/override yields a **stale module** from `lru_cache`.

## Unknowns
- Whether `cut --render` is exercised anywhere in prod (if not, the bug is latent); whether `iteration.mp4.provenance.json` is consumed downstream.

## Risks
- Four live bypasses escape the registry/CLI abstraction: iteration_video in-process import, iteration plan module spawn, cut/human_notes `executor_argv` module spawns, tests' in-process imports.
- Stale provenance after `hype.mp4→iteration.mp4` rename silently breaks provenance chaining.

## Suggested approach
1. Fix cut/resume to call `rendering.executors.render.run.render` (correct dotted import) or drop `--render`.
2. Route iteration_video through `astrid executors run rendering.render` (or `executor_argv("rendering.render", …)`), and rename/rewrite the provenance sidecar alongside the mp4.
3. Make `resolve_executor_runtime_module` cache-aware of registry changes (bust on override/fork) or key by registry generation.
